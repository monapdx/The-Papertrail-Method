**RECEIPT**

An itemized record of a retail transaction.

- RETAILER
- ITEMS (item and dollar amount for each entry)
- SUBTOTAL (optional)
- TOTAL
- TRANSACTION NUMBER
- PAYMENT TYPE (optional)

List each item with its dollar amount. SUBTOTAL and PAYMENT TYPE are optional. All other fields are required.
