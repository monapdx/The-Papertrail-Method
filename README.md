 ## **The Papertrail Method**  

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/monapdx/The-Papertrail-Method/refs/heads/main/Images/The%20Papertrail%20Method.png">

---

<img src="Images/paper-plane.gif" alt="Paper plane" align="left" width="150">

 _Build the system. Follow the paper trail._

A reusable writing method for creating stories through the records people and organizations produce: submissions, emails, schedules, scripts, reports, and more. Define who receives information, what they can do with it, and what happens next. Each action leaves a trace—and moves the story forward.

The result is **systemic fiction**: storytelling through the workings of an imagined organization, whether it produces a podcast, television show, radio broadcast, or news edition. The approach is especially suited to satire, parody, and mystery, where what gets documented, distorted, or concealed becomes part of the story.

Readers discover the world and its characters by following the traces they leave behind.

## CONTENT TYPES




| TYPE                  | DEFINITION                                                              | **CATEGORY**          | PURPOSE                                                                                |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **[EPISODE](Content-Types/EPISODE.md)**       | An installment in a show                                                | **[PUBLICATION](../Content-Categories/PUBLICATION.md)**   | Provides the transcript for an episode's content                                       |
| **[FORM](FORM.md)**          | A web form with input fields + account destination                      | **[INTERACTIVE](../Content-Categories/INTERACTIVE.md)**   | Collects information from someone                                                      |
| **[SUBMISSION](SUBMISSION.md)**    | Form input responses sent as an EMAIL                                   | **[COMMUNICATION](../Content-Categories/COMMUNICATION.md)** | Creates the first EMAIL in a THREAD                                                    |
| **[EMAIL](EMAIL.md)**         | A message that gets sent to a character's ACCOUNT                       | **[COMMUNICATION](../Content-Categories/COMMUNICATION.md)** | Communication between characters                                                       |
| **[ACCOUNT](ACCOUNT.md)**       | Email interface belonging to a CHARACTER                                | **[COLLECTION](../Content-Categories/COLLECTION.md)**    | Accept submissions, forward submissions, reply to submissions, internal correspondence |
| **[THREAD](THREAD.md)**        | A sequence of EMAILS that begin with a SUBMISSION                       | **[COLLECTION](../Content-Categories/COLLECTION.md)**    | Allows readers to see all emails regarding a submission grouped together in sequence   |
| **[CHAT](CHAT.md)**          | A conversation between characters                                       | **[COMMUNICATION](../Content-Categories/COMMUNICATION.md)** | A simulated interactive chat conversation between characters                           |
| **[INTERVIEW](INTERVIEW.md)**     | A type of EPISODE transcript between a HOST and a GUEST                 | **[PUBLICATION](../Content-Categories/PUBLICATION.md)**   | Specifies the type of EPISODE by introducing a guest speaker                           |
| **[ADVERTISEMENT](ADVERTISEMENT.md)** | An advertisement for a business or organization aired during an EPISODE | **[PUBLICATION](../Content-Categories/PUBLICATION.md)**   | Establishes a business, organization, product, or service that sponsors the show       |
| **[FLOWCHART](FLOWCHART.md)**     | An artifact that shows relationships between DECISIONS and OUTCOMES     | **[ARTIFACT](../Content-Categories/ARTIFACT.md)**      | Guides the reader through the decision making process of the flowchart author          |
| **[SPREADSHEET](SPREADSHEET.md)**   | An artifact that can contain data entries in a table format             | **[ARTIFACT](../Content-Categories/ARTIFACT.md)**      | Allows the reader to see what information is collected and how it is categorized       |
| **[BUSINESS CARD](BUSINESS%20CARD.md)** | An artifact that contains information about a character                 | **[ARTIFACT](../Content-Categories/ARTIFACT.md)**      | Establishes character identities and information about their roles                     |
| **[DOCUMENT](DOCUMENT.md)**      | An general purpose artifact containing any text or content              | **[ARTIFACT](../Content-Categories/ARTIFACT.md)**      | Acts as a container for content that isn't specified in any other available format     |
| **[CHARACTER](CHARACTER.md)**     | A character in a story                                                  | **[IDENTITY](../Content-Categories/IDENTITY.md)**      | Anchors every artifact and communication to an established identity                    |
| **[ORGANIZATION](ORGANIZATION.md)**  | The fictional organization that your story is told through              | **[IDENTITY](../Content-Categories/IDENTITY.md)**      | The main storytelling engine for the entire method                                     |
