01 / The method

A world told through what it leaves behind.

A reusable writing method for creating stories through the records people and organizations produce: submissions, emails, schedules, scripts, reports, and more. Define who receives information, what they can do with it, and what happens next. Each action leaves a trace—and moves the story forward.

The result is systemic fiction: storytelling through the workings of an imagined organization, whether it produces a podcast, television show, radio broadcast, or news edition. The approach is especially suited to satire, parody, and mystery, where what gets documented, distorted, or concealed becomes part of the story.

Readers discover the world and its characters by following the traces they leave behind.
